Domain Knowledge : 
1. budget:
The estimated amount of money spent on producing the movie, usually measured in US dollars (USD).

2. genres:
The categories of the movie, such as Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, or Romance. A movie can belong to multiple genres.

3. homepage:
The official website URL of the movie, if available. Missing values mean no website is recorded.

4. movie_id:
A unique identifier assigned to each movie. It helps distinguish movies but usually has no predictive meaning.

5. keywords:
Words or phrases describing the movie's themes, subjects, or important concepts.

6. original_language:
The language in which the movie was originally produced, such as English, French, or Japanese.

7. original_title:
The movie's original title in its original language. It may differ from its internationally released title.

8. overview:
A short textual description or summary of the movie's plot. It can be useful for Natural Language Processing (NLP).

9. popularity:
A popularity score assigned by the dataset's source. It may reflect audience interest and online activity. It is not necessarily a measure of movie quality.

10. production_companies:
The companies involved in producing the movie, such as major film studios.

11. production_countries:
The countries associated with the movie's production.

12. release_date:
The date when the movie was released. It can be used to extract the release year, month, or season.

13. revenue:
The total reported income generated by the movie, usually measured in US dollars (USD).

14. runtime:
The duration of the movie, usually measured in minutes.

15. spoken_languages:
The languages spoken in the movie. A movie can contain multiple languages.

16. status:
The movie's production or release status, such as Released or Post Production.

17. tagline:
A short promotional phrase used to advertise the movie.

18. title_x:
The movie's title as recorded in the dataset. It may be the same as original_title or represent a localized or commonly used title.

19. vote_average:
The average audience rating for the movie, typically on a scale of 0 to 10 in this dataset.

20. vote_count:
The number of audience votes contributing to the movie's rating. A higher count provides more rating observations.

21. cast:
Information about the actors and actresses who performed in the movie, potentially including their character names.

22. crew:
Information about people working behind the scenes, such as directors, writers, producers, and editors.
